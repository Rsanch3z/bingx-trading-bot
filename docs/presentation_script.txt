[SLIDE 3 — Perpetual Futures 101]

"Before the strategies: what is a perpetual future, and why does it change everything about costs?"
（在講策略之前：什麼是永續期貨，它為什麼會徹底改變費用結構？）

"A perpetual future is a contract — you never own the actual coin. You are simply betting on whether the price goes up or down. The key difference from spot trading: you can use leverage. We used 20x, meaning we control twenty dollars of position for every one dollar of our own capital."
（永續期貨是一種合約——你從來不擁有實際的幣。你只是在賭價格漲或跌。與現貨交易的關鍵區別：你可以使用槓桿。我們使用 20 倍槓桿，意味著每 1 美元的本金控制 20 美元的倉位。）

"You can go LONG if you expect prices to rise, or SHORT if you expect them to fall. Both directions are available."
（如果你預期價格上漲就做多，如果預期下跌就做空。兩個方向都可以交易。）

"Now here is the cost problem. Opening and closing one trade costs 0.10% of the notional value in taker fees. With 20x leverage, that is 2% of your actual capital — just to get in and out. Plus a funding rate of 0.01% every 8 hours. A strategy that makes many short trades is simply paying fees to the exchange."
（現在是費用問題。開倉和平倉一次要支付名義金額 0.10% 的吃單手續費。在 20 倍槓桿下，這是你實際本金的 2%——僅僅是進出一次。加上每 8 小時 0.01% 的資金費率。頻繁交易的策略基本上就是在替交易所打工。）

[SLIDE 5 — Six Strategies, Six Philosophies]

"Our six strategies each represent a different philosophy about how markets work."
（我們的六種策略各自代表一種對市場運作的不同理解。）

"EMA Cross: follow the momentum. When the fast average crosses the slow average, a trend has begun. MACD Cross: confirm before you commit — two indicators must agree."
（EMA 交叉：追隨動量。當快速均線穿越慢速均線，趨勢已開始。MACD 交叉：進場前先確認——兩個指標必須一致。）

"Bollinger Bands: the market oscillates. Buy when statistically cheap, sell when statistically expensive. SR Breakout: wait for structure — let price break a real support or resistance level, then enter."
（布林帶：市場會震盪。在統計上便宜時買入，昂貴時賣出。S/R 突破：等待結構——讓價格突破真正的支撐或阻力，再進場。）

"BB Squeeze: catch the explosion. When volatility compresses to a minimum, a big move is coming — enter the moment it fires. EMA Pullback: enter at value, not at exhaustion. Wait for a pullback to EMA-21 in a confirmed trend, and buy when buyers defend that level."
（BB Squeeze：捕捉爆發。當波動性壓縮到最低點，大行情就要來了——在它啟動的瞬間進場。EMA 回調：在價值點進場，而非在頂部追高。在確認的趨勢中等待價格回調到 EMA-21，當買家在那個位置守住時進場。）

[SLIDE 6 — What Are These Indicators?]

"Let me take ninety seconds to explain the core indicators so the rest of the results make sense."
（讓我用 90 秒解釋核心指標，這樣接下來的結果才容易理解。）

"EMA — Exponential Moving Average. A smoothed price line that gives more weight to recent candles. When price is above EMA, we are in an uptrend. When a fast EMA crosses a slow EMA, a momentum shift is happening."
（EMA——指數移動平均線。一條給近期K棒更大權重的平滑價格線。當價格在 EMA 上方，我們處於上升趨勢。當快速 EMA 穿越慢速 EMA，動量正在轉換。）

"MACD measures the gap between two EMAs. When the gap is growing, momentum is accelerating. When the histogram flips sign, momentum has reversed."
（MACD 測量兩條 EMA 之間的差距。當差距擴大，動量在加速。當柱狀圖改變正負號，動量已反轉。）

"Bollinger Bands draw two statistical lines — two standard deviations above and below a 20-period average. When price touches the lower band, it is statistically unusual — the market is stretched. RSI below 35 confirms: momentum is also stretched downward. Two confirmations means higher probability."
（布林帶繪製兩條統計線——20 期平均線上下各兩個標準差。當價格觸及下軌，在統計上是不尋常的——市場被拉伸了。RSI 低於 35 確認：動量也在向下拉伸。兩個確認意味著更高的概率。）

"ATR — Average True Range — measures how many dollars a coin typically moves per candle. BTC moves around $400 per hour candle. XRP moves about $0.004. We use ATR to size stop losses so they adapt to each coin's actual volatility — no fixed percentages."
（ATR——真實波幅均值——衡量一種幣每根K棒通常移動多少美元。BTC 每根小時K棒移動約 400 美元。XRP 大約移動 0.004 美元。我們用 ATR 來設定止損大小，使其適應每種幣的實際波動性——不使用固定百分比。）

[SLIDE 7 — Why We Added Two More Strategies Mid-Project]

"After the first round of backtesting, we added two new strategies because the original four had a shared weakness: they all entered at crossovers — random points mid-trend."
（第一輪回測後，我們新增了兩個策略，因為原有的四個策略有一個共同弱點：它們都在交叉點進場——趨勢中間的隨機位置。）

"BB Squeeze solves a different problem: when markets compress into tight ranges, they are storing energy. The Bollinger Band width hitting a 30-bar minimum tells us the coil is tightest right now. When width expands again — the coil releases. We enter the first expansion candle, confirmed by MACD direction."
（BB Squeeze 解決了一個不同的問題：當市場壓縮成緊密區間時，它們在儲存能量。布林帶寬度達到 30 根K棒的最低點告訴我們彈簧現在壓得最緊。當寬度再次擴大——彈簧釋放。我們在第一根擴張K棒進場，由 MACD 方向確認。）

"EMA Pullback solves the chasing problem. Instead of buying when EMA-9 crosses EMA-21 — which is often at the top of a mini-move — we wait for the pullback to EMA-21 itself. Then we require three things: ADX above 22 confirms a real trend, the candle low touches EMA-21, and the candle closes back above EMA-21 with a bullish body. That closing candle is proof that buyers stepped in at exactly that support level."
（EMA Pullback 解決了追價問題。不是在 EMA-9 穿越 EMA-21 時買入——那通常是在小幅波動的頂部——而是等待回調到 EMA-21 本身。然後我們需要三個條件：ADX 高於 22 確認真正的趨勢，K棒低點觸及 EMA-21，以及K棒以陽線收盤並收回 EMA-21 上方。那根收盤K棒是買家在那個支撐位入場的證明。）

[SLIDE 8 — Three Techniques That Changed the Results]

"In the second round of upgrades, we added three techniques that did more to improve results than any new strategy."
（在第二輪升級中，我們加入了三項技術，它們對結果的改善超過了任何新策略。）

"ADX — Average Directional Index. It measures trend strength from 0 to 100, direction-neutral. ADX above 22 means the market is trending — use EMA or MACD. ADX below 20 means the market is ranging and choppy — use BB or BB Squeeze. ADX between 20 and 22 is ambiguous — skip the trade entirely. This gate alone prevented hundreds of wrong-regime entries."
（ADX——平均方向指數。它衡量 0 到 100 的趨勢強度，與方向無關。ADX 高於 22 表示市場在趨勢中——使用 EMA 或 MACD。ADX 低於 20 表示市場在盤整——使用 BB 或 BB Squeeze。ADX 在 20 到 22 之間是模糊的——完全跳過這筆交易。這個門控單獨阻止了數百次錯誤市場狀態下的進場。）

"Chandelier Exit: a trailing stop that can only move in the profitable direction. Formula: stop equals peak price minus 2.5 times ATR. As the price climbs, the stop ratchets up. When price drops to the stop, the trade closes. You never give back a large gain — the stop is always anchored to the highest point reached."
（Chandelier Exit：一個只能向獲利方向移動的跟蹤止損。公式：止損等於最高價減去 2.5 倍 ATR。隨著價格攀升，止損向上棘輪。當價格跌至止損，交易平倉。你永遠不會還回大筆收益——止損始終錨定在到達過的最高點。）

"Two-Stage Exit: instead of binary all-or-nothing outcomes, we split the trade. Fifty percent exits at plus 2R, immediately locking profit and moving the stop to breakeven. The remaining half uses Chandelier Exit to chase the full move. Worst case after stage one: you break even on the second half. Best case: you ride a 5R or 8R move with half your position still on."
（二段式出場：不是全有或全無的二元結果，我們把交易分割。50% 在 +2R 出場，立即鎖定利潤並將止損移至保本。另一半使用 Chandelier Exit 追逐完整行情。第一階段後的最壞情況：你在第二半保本。最好情況：你用一半倉位騎完 5R 或 8R 的行情。）

[SLIDE 10 — Two Ways to Generate Signals]

"Thank you. I will explain the technical architecture — how a candle becomes an order."
（謝謝。我來解釋技術架構——一根K棒是如何變成一筆訂單的。）

"We implemented two signal paths. Path A: TradingView sends an alert via webhook to a Google Cloud Run endpoint, which queues the signal to Pub/Sub, which our bot pulls and executes. Path B — what we actually use — is fully self-contained: the bot fetches candles directly from the BingX API, runs all indicator math locally, generates the signal, and places the order. No cloud needed. One Python command."
（我們實現了兩種信號路徑。路徑 A：TradingView 通過 webhook 發送警報到 Google Cloud Run 端點，將信號排隊到 Pub/Sub，我們的機器人拉取並執行。路徑 B——我們實際使用的——完全自包含：機器人直接從 BingX API 獲取K棒，在本地運行所有指標計算，生成信號，並下單。不需要雲服務。一個 Python 指令。）

"The system has seven components, all cleanly separated."

[SLIDE 12 — Position Sizing]

"How do we decide how large a position to take? This is where most retail traders make their biggest mistake."
（我們如何決定倉位大小？這是大多數散戶犯最大錯誤的地方。）

"Our rule: risk exactly 1 percent of current equity per trade. No more, no exceptions. With $10,000 equity, that is $100 of risk per trade."
（我們的規則：每筆交易精確冒當前資金的 1%。不多，沒有例外。10,000 美元資金，每筆交易的風險是 100 美元。）

"The formula: position size equals risk amount divided by stop loss distance. If the stop is 0.5 percent away, position notional equals $100 divided by 0.5 percent equals $20,000. With 20x leverage, that uses $1,000 of actual margin — 10 percent of equity. The effective leverage is 2x, far below the 20x cap."
（公式：倉位大小等於風險金額除以止損距離。如果止損在 0.5% 之外，名義倉位等於 100 美元除以 0.5% 等於 20,000 美元。在 20 倍槓桿下，這使用了 1,000 美元的實際保證金——資金的 10%。有效槓桿是 2 倍，遠低於 20 倍上限。）

"Critical insight: with risk-based sizing, if I lose 10 consecutive trades, I lose only 10 percent of my account. I cannot blow up."
（關鍵洞察：使用風險比例倉位管理，如果我連輸 10 筆，我只損失 10% 的賬戶。我不可能爆倉。）

"We also added volatility-adjusted sizing: when ATR-percent is above 1.5 times its own 50-bar rolling mean — meaning the market is unusually volatile right now — we automatically halve the position. Normal risk when calm; automatic de-risk during spikes."
（我們還加入了波動性調整倉位：當 ATR 百分比超過其自身 50 根K棒滾動均值的 1.5 倍——意味著市場現在異常波動——我們自動將倉位減半。平靜時正常風險；波動峰值時自動降低風險。）

[SLIDE 13 — Cost Model]

"Before any strategy makes money, it has to survive two types of costs."
（在任何策略賺錢之前，它必須先承受兩種費用。）

"Taker fee: 0.05 percent per side on notional. Open plus close equals 0.10 percent round trip. With 20x leverage, that is 2 percent of capital per trade. If a strategy takes 50 trades in 2 months, fees alone consume 10 to 15 percent of equity before a single profitable dollar."
（吃單費：名義金額每側 0.05%。開倉加平倉等於 0.10% 的來回。在 20 倍槓桿下，每筆交易是本金的 2%。如果一個策略在 2 個月內進行 50 筆交易，僅費用就在賺到任何利潤之前消耗了 10 到 15% 的資金。）

"Funding rate: 0.01 percent of notional every 8 hours. This is charged or received depending on market direction. For long positions in a bull market, you typically pay. For short positions, you may receive. We model this precisely for every trade."
（資金費率：每 8 小時名義金額的 0.01%。根據市場方向收取或支付。在牛市中的多頭倉位，你通常支付。做空倉位，你可能收取。我們對每筆交易精確模擬這一點。）

"This cost structure is why our 7-month results show such a large gap between the best and worst strategies. It is not just about win rate — it is about how many trades you take to get those wins."
（這種費用結構解釋了為什麼我們的 7 個月結果在最佳和最差策略之間有如此大的差距。這不只是關於勝率——也關於你需要多少筆交易才能獲得這些勝利。）

[SLIDE 14 — Risk Controls & API Security]

"Three layers of protection, both financial and technical."
（三層保護，包括財務和技術兩方面。）

"Financial: one percent max risk per trade. Daily circuit breaker halts all new trades if daily loss reaches minus 5 percent. Maximum 7 open positions simultaneously across all symbols."
（財務方面：每筆交易最大風險 1%。如果每日損失達到負 5%，每日斷路器停止所有新交易。所有幣種同時最多 7 個開倉。）

"Technical: API keys stored in a dot-env file, never committed to GitHub. Permissions on BingX API key: Read and Perpetual Futures Trading only. Withdrawals and transfers explicitly disabled — even if someone steals the key, they cannot touch the funds."

Q: Why does direction bias make such a huge difference?
（為什麼方向性偏向有如此巨大的差異？）

"In a bull market, most random entries will go long. In a bear market, most random entries will go short. If your strategy fires equally in both directions in a trending market, half of your trades are automatically fighting the trend. Those trades have lower win rates and get stopped out faster. Filtering to only the trend direction removes the worst half of your trade population while keeping the best half. The win rate naturally rises."
（在牛市中，大多數隨機進場都會做多。在熊市中，大多數隨機進場都會做空。如果你的策略在趨勢市場中兩個方向都同等觸發，一半的交易自動在對抗趨勢。那些交易的勝率更低，止損更快被觸發。過濾只跟趨勢方向交易，消除了你交易群體中最差的一半，同時保留最好的一半。勝率自然上升。）

（S/R 突破進場條件在比較進場價格與已經低於當前價格的阻力位。意思是：我們在尋找一個我們已經通過的水平的突破。條件永遠不可能為真。策略幾天內返回零交易，我們以為它在正確地選擇性——但它無聲地壞掉了。修復只需要一行，但找到它需要幾個小時在每個過濾階段添加調試打印。）
