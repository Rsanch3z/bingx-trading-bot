Speaker 1 · Introduction

What Are These Indicators?

EMA — Exponential Moving Average

A weighted price average where recent candles count more. Tracks the direction the market is trending. When a fast EMA (9) crosses a slow EMA (21), it signals a potential trend change. EMA(50) tells us the overall trend direction.

MACD — Moving Avg Convergence Divergence

Measures the gap between two EMAs. When the MACD line crosses its signal line, it confirms a momentum shift. Positive histogram = upward momentum building. Slower than EMA cross → fewer, higher-quality signals.

Bollinger Bands (BB)

A price channel set at ±2 standard deviations from a 20-period moving average. When price touches the band edges, it is statistically "unusual" — the market is likely to mean-revert back toward center. Wider bands = higher volatility.

RSI — Relative Strength Index

Measures momentum on a 0–100 scale. Above 70 = overbought (likely to fall). Below 30 = oversold (likely to rise). We use RSI as a second confirmation — only enter BB signals when RSI also confirms the extreme.

ATR (Average True Range) — measures how many dollars a coin typically moves per candle. BTC: ~$400/candle, XRP: ~$0.004. We size all stop losses as a multiple of ATR so they adapt to each coin's actual volatility.
Speaker 1 · New Strategies

Why We Added Two More Strategies Mid-Project

Volatility Breakout
BB Squeeze — Catch the Explosion

Markets breathe: periods of compression (low volatility, narrow BB) alternate with expansion (big moves). The squeeze detects when BB width hits a 30-bar minimum — the market is coiling. When width expands again, a big move has begun.

Entry: first candle expanding out of the squeeze, confirmed by MACD histogram direction. EMA(200) macro gate applies.

Why it works: low volatility reliably precedes large moves. You enter at the start of the explosion — maximum R:R potential — not after the move is obvious.
Trend Continuation
EMA Pullback — Buy the Dip Properly

Problem with crossover strategies: they enter at random points mid-trend, often at exhaustion highs. EMA Pullback waits for price to retrace to EMA(21) — the short-term dynamic support — then confirms buyers stepped back in.

Entry requires: ADX > 22 (trend confirmed) + candle low touches EMA(21) + candle closes back above EMA(21) with a bullish body. All three must align.

Why it works: enter at value (structural support) with a confirmation that buyers defended the level — not at a random crossover signal.
Speaker 1 · Advanced Techniques

Three Techniques That Changed the Results

Regime Detection
ADX — Average Directional Index

Measures trend strength on a 0–100 scale — direction-neutral. High ADX = strong trend (either direction). Low ADX = choppy/ranging market.

ADX Value Market State Use
> 22 Trending EMA / MACD
20 – 22 Ambiguous Skip trade
< 20 Ranging/Choppy BB / Squeeze

Classic mistake: trend strategy in a ranging market = constant whipsaws. ADX gate prevents this at source.

Volatility Trailing
Chandelier Exit

A trailing stop that locks in new peaks and can only move in the profitable direction. 

For LONG:
stop = peak_price − 2.5 × ATR

For SHORT:
stop = trough + 2.5 × ATR

As price rises, stop ratchets up. When price finally drops to the stop, trade closes. Named after a chandelier hanging from the ceiling. Never moves backward against you.

Blended Exit
Two-Stage Exit

Replaces binary "hit TP or hit SL" with a two-part plan:

  • Stage 1: 50% position exits at +2R — profit locked, stop moves to breakeven
  • Stage 2: remaining 50% trails with Chandelier Exit to capture the full move

Worst case after Stage 1: you breakeven on the second half. Best case: you ride a 5–10R move with half position.

Combined effect: win rates 20–28% → 39–56%  |  total fee drag 10–27% → 1.5–8%  |  max drawdowns cut by half on best strategies.

Position Sizing — Risk-Based with Leverage

The formula

# Kelly criterion (simplified)
Kelly%  = win_rate − loss_rate / RR
Risk    = equity × Kelly%
         (capped at 1% max)

# Notional position
Notional = Risk / SL_distance
         (capped at 20× equity)

Worked example

Variable Value
Equity 10,000 USDT
Kelly → risk 1% → 100 USDT
SL distance 1%
Notional 10,000 USDT (1× equity)
Effective leverage 1× (far below 20× cap)
Higher leverage ≠ more risk per trade. Each losing trade still costs exactly 1% of equity.
Speaker 2 · Architecture

Cost Model — Every Trade Has Two Fees

Fee types

  • Taker fee: 0.05% per side on notional
    Open + close = 0.10% round-trip
  • Funding rate: 0.01% per 8 h on notional
    Longs pay shorts — or reverse if rate goes negative
Over 8 trades on one symbol: ~0.8–1.1% of equity goes to fees before any profit.

Calculation in code

# Fee: open + close
fee = 2 × 0.0005 × position_size

# Funding: prorated by hold time
hold_8h = (exit_ms − entry_ms)
          ÷ (8 × 3,600,000)
funding = hold_8h × 0.0001
          × position_size

# Shorts receive funding (negative)
if side == SHORT:
    funding = −funding

net_pnl = gross − fee − funding

What Changed: 6 Upgraded Techniques

Two-Stage Exit + Chandelier

50% of position exits at +2R — locks in profit immediately. The trailing half uses Chandelier Exit (ATR×2.5) to ride the rest of the move. Replaces simple 1R trailing stop.

ADX(14) Regime Gate

ADX > 22 → trending market → use MACD. ADX < 20 → ranging market → use BB. ADX 20–22 → ambiguous → skip trade entirely. Removes wrong-regime entries at source.

4H Multi-Timeframe Confirmation

EMA(50) calculated on aggregated 4H candles must agree with the 1H signal direction. Filters out 1H setups that conflict with the bigger trend.

Session Filter (MACD)

MACD entries only during 08:00–20:00 UTC, Mon–Fri. Avoids low-liquidity Asian overnight sessions and weekend crypto pump/dump patterns.

Volatility-Adjusted Risk Sizing

When ATR% > 1.5× its own 50-bar rolling mean, position size is halved. Normal 1% equity risk in calm markets; automatic de-risking in volatile spikes.

EMA(200) Buffer ±0.5%

No trades within 0.5% of the EMA(200) line. Avoids the choppy indecision zone where trend direction is genuinely ambiguous and false signals cluster.

Speaker 3 · Profitability Framework

3 Keys to Long-Term Profitability

What 7 months of backtesting taught us — each key is necessary, none is sufficient alone

Key 1
Regime Detection
Use the right tool for the market

Trending market → use EMA / MACD / EMA Pullback. Ranging market → use BB / BB Squeeze. Using a trend strategy when ADX is low causes constant whipsaws. Using BB when ADX is high gets stopped out on every breakout.

ADX > 22 → trend strategy
ADX < 20 → mean reversion
20–22 → no trade
Key 2
Direction Bias
Only trade WITH the macro trend

In a bull market, only go long. In a bear market, only go short. Use EMA(200) slope and EMA(50) slope to confirm the macro direction. Ignore countertrend signals entirely — they add losses, not opportunity.

SOL SR+MACD both-sides: −2.63%
SOL SR+MACD short-only: +19.61%
Same strategy. $22 difference on $100.
Key 3
Exit Mastery
Lock profit early, let winners run

The entry is only half the trade. Two-stage exit locks 50% at +2R immediately — you've already won on that half. Chandelier Exit trails the rest, letting a +2R trade become +5R or +8R without giving back the locked portion.

Simple TP/SL: all-or-nothing
Two-stage: partial win always secured
Max drawdown on XRP BB: 2.2%
The formula: pick the right strategy for the regime → trade only with the macro direction → exit in two stages to lock and run. All three together: SOL SR+MACD short-only in bear = +19.61%, dd 2.4%, 55% win rate, 55 trades.
Speaker 4 · Demo & Conclusions

What We Actually Learned

Strategy autopsies — updated results

  • EMA (−3.36% bear, −5.72% bull): 4H MTF + EMA(200) buffer cuts noise. ETH +9.28% in bear — two-stage exit captured the full move. Still negative avg without regime bias.
  • MACD (−2.18% bear, −11.70% bull): Session filter + ADX gate improved bear significantly. Still worst in bull — fires against the bull trend.
  • BB (+0.34% bear, +4.57% bull long-only): ADX ranging gate unlocks BB's edge. First consistently positive strategy. BNB +19.15% in bull unfiltered.
  • SR+MACD bear short-only: +1.92% — best bear strategy with directional bias. BTC short-only +12.79%.

The five real lessons

  • Direction is the biggest lever. SOL SR+MACD short-only +19.61% vs both-sides −2.63%. XRP BB_Squeeze long-only +11.60% vs both-sides +11.37%. Same strategy, right direction.
  • Exit design matters as much as entry. Two-stage exit locked profits; Chandelier trailed winners. Avg cost cut from 10–27% → 4–7%.

Speaker 4 · Conclusions

Limitations — What This Backtest Cannot Tell Us

  • Two windows may not generalize. We tested one bear period (Apr–May 2025, 83 days) and one bull period (Nov 2024–Jan 2025, 83 days). More windows and more symbols needed for statistical confidence.
  • No slippage modelled. Real market orders get slightly worse prices than the theoretical TP/SL. We assumed exact execution.
  • Funding rate assumed constant. BingX funding ranges from −0.03% to +0.10% per 8h. We used the standard 0.01% estimate.
  • One position per symbol. A real portfolio would need to handle correlation between BTC and ETH moving together.
  • No parameter optimization. We did not test whether EMA(9,21) beats EMA(10,20) or (12,26). The published parameters are textbook defaults, not optimized for crypto.
